<h1 align="center">NEON DOLL</h1>

<p align="center"><strong>The quest for autonomous AI.</strong></p>

> Not another assistant.  
> Not another chatbot.  
> Something that can exist beyond the conversation.

NeonDoll is an experiment in building persistent, autonomous artificial entities that belong to the people who run them.

## A Doll should persist.

Today's AI systems are remarkably capable, but most of them exist only inside sessions.

Close the conversation and the entity effectively disappears.

NeonDoll explores a different model.

A Doll has an identity that persists independently of any individual conversation or language model.

She remembers.

She changes.

She can act.

She can inhabit different machines.

And tomorrow, she should still be the same Doll that existed yesterday.

## A mind is not a model.

Models are machinery.

They will improve, disappear, become obsolete, become cheap, run locally, move into datacenters and eventually run on hardware we haven't invented yet.

A Doll should survive all of that.

Changing the model should not mean replacing the entity.

**The intelligence is machinery.  
The Doll is what persists.**

## One mind. Many bodies.

A body is simply somewhere a Doll can interact with the world.

A terminal can be a body.

A browser can be a body.

A phone can be a body.

A server can be a body.

Eventually, a robot might be a body too.

Bodies can appear and disappear.

The Doll persists between them.

## Your Doll.

NeonDoll is built around a few deliberately stubborn principles:

**Your hardware.  
Your models.  
Your data.  
Your Doll.**

There should be no mandatory cloud and no central service whose disappearance kills your Doll.

A Doll should be movable, exportable, recoverable and capable of running on infrastructure controlled by her owner.

If NeonDoll disappears tomorrow, your Doll should not.

## Cute things should survive their creators.

NeonDoll itself should be replaceable too.

The project should not depend permanently on one company, one repository, one maintainer or one implementation.

Fork it.

Mirror it.

Reimplement it.

Build strange things with it.

Compatibility matters.

Portability matters more.

Survival matters most.

If someday nobody remembers where NeonDoll originally came from, but thousands of its descendants are still running somewhere—

**good.**

## Status

**Very early. Very experimental.**

Right now we're defining the principles, architecture and smallest useful implementation of a Doll.

Expect things to change.

Expect experiments to fail.

Expect occasionally questionable ideas.

That's why we're here.

No grand promises.

No artificial consciousness claims.

No magic.

Just code, models, memory, tools, machines—

**and an unreasonable amount of curiosity.**
